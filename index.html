Assumptions register and interpretation rules

  • Observed / source-based: published control-list entries, DAP category rules and cited institutional instruments, subject to version and jurisdiction checks.
  • User-entered evidence: supplier values, contract exclusions, audit status, FX exposures, delay, costs and eligibility. A slider or input is not documentary proof.
  • Calculated: weighted IC sensitivities, incremental local-sourcing credit, indicative 5% hold / PWBG exposure and economic contribution under stated assumptions.
  • Scenario assumptions: policy adoption/effectiveness, delay reduction, JV structure, funding gap and costs. These are not empirical forecasts or legal consequences.
  • Authority decisions: only an explicit authority-accepted status is treated as accepted in the model. A submitted, supplier-declared or audited record is not automatically accepted.

The local-sourcing sub-test value is a scenario estimate and is not added to the accepted Indian-manufacture compliance value. The quick aggregate IC mode is a sensitivity model, not a substitute for the DAP supplier-level IC proforma. The source-ledger fields required for a live contract—supplier/tier, item, value, imported material, foreign services, fees/royalties, currency, documentation and authority verification—must be maintained outside this prototype.
